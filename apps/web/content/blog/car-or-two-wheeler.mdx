A bay that fits a scooter does not always fit a hatchback, and a driveway that takes one car might take three two-wheelers. So ParkEase never treats them as the same thing.

## Two lists, two prices

When an owner lists a space, they say how many car slots and how many two-wheeler slots it has, and set an hourly price for each. A basement might offer two car bays at ₹40 an hour and four two-wheeler bays at ₹15. Those are separate slots, booked separately, and the owner keeps the same share of either.

## Why the map changes when you switch

The map only shows spaces with a slot free for the vehicle you have selected. Switch from car to two-wheeler and some pins disappear while others appear, because the count of free slots is worked out per vehicle type. A green pin always means a slot that fits what you drive is free right now.

## Adding your vehicle

Add the vehicles you drive once, with their registration numbers. When you book, you pick which one you are bringing, and that is what the owner sees when they scan you in. If you switch between a car and a scooter during the week, keep both on your account.

## For owners

If your space can take both, list both. Two-wheeler slots usually rent for less per hour but fill up more often, and they fit into corners a car cannot use. You can change the counts and prices whenever you like; bookings already made keep the price they were made at.
