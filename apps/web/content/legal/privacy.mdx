## Who else handles it

We share data only with processors that help us run ParkEase, and only what each needs:

- **Razorpay:** payments, refunds and payouts, including payout bank details and PAN.
- **Firebase (Google):** phone sign-in and push notifications.
- **Cloudinary:** storage and delivery of photos.
- **Sentry:** error monitoring, to find and fix crashes.
- **Upstash:** short-lived caching that keeps the app fast.
