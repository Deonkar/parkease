## What we collect

- **Account details:** your mobile number, name and, if you give it, your email address.
- **Vehicles:** the vehicle type and registration number of each vehicle you add, and optionally its make, model and colour.
- **Location:** your location while you use the map to find parking, and the location of spaces owners list. [DRAFT: confirm whether location is stored or only used in the request.]
- **Bookings and payments:** what you booked, when, what you paid and any refunds. Card and UPI details go to Razorpay and are never stored by ParkEase.
- **Owners and partners:** payout details and PAN, which are collected and held by Razorpay for payouts, and the identity documents valets and car wash partners provide when they register.
- **Photos you upload:** listing photos, and the before-and-after photos car wash partners take as proof of a wash.
- **Reviews and messages** you write in the app, and the reports you make.
- **Device and diagnostic data:** a notification token for your device, and error reports when the app crashes.

## Who else handles it

We share data only with processors that help us run ParkEase, and only what each needs:

- **Razorpay:** payments, refunds and payouts, including payout bank details and PAN.
- **Firebase (Google):** phone sign-in and push notifications.
- **Cloudinary:** storage and delivery of photos.
- **Sentry:** error monitoring, to find and fix crashes.
- **Upstash:** short-lived caching that keeps the app fast.
