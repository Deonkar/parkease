What happens to your money when a parking booking is cancelled, by you or by the space owner. The same rules are built into the app, so the refund it shows you is the refund you get.

## When you cancel

- **Before your start time:** a full refund, less a ₹{PRICING.refunds.processingFee} processing fee. If the booking cost less than ₹{PRICING.refunds.processingFee}, nothing is refunded and nothing more is charged.
- **Within {PRICING.refunds.graceMinutes} minutes of your start time:** {PRICING.refunds.gracePercent}% of what you paid.
- **More than {PRICING.refunds.graceMinutes} minutes after your start time:** no refund, because the space was held for you.

## When the owner cancels

A full refund, at any time, even in the middle of a booking. ParkEase also pays you ₹{PRICING.refunds.ownerCancelGoodwill} for the trouble. It is paid by ParkEase, never taken from the owner or anyone else. The same applies if ParkEase cancels a booking.

## Valet call-outs

If you are not there {PRICING.valet.noShowGraceMinutes} minutes after your valet arrives, the ₹{PRICING.valet.base} call-out fee is charged and the distance charge is not.
